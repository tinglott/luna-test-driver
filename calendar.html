Test a booking

Bookings are made inside a test call: open Talk to Luna, start a call, and say “I want to book a check-up on Monday at 10am”. The receptionist holds the slot, you confirm, and a real event is created on the test calendar — then deleted automatically when your test call ends. Nothing stays booked.

First-time callers cannot book directly — the owner approves those on the front desk page.
