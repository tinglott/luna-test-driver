What Luna does for your business

One package, five services, one price:

  • Answering — Luna answers the calls you miss, politely and professionally, using only the facts you gave us about your business. It never guesses, never invents a price, and never pretends to be a person.
  • Scheduling — callers can book an appointment on the call, in plain conversation. Luna checks your real availability first, so it never double-books and never books when you are closed.
  • Calendar — every booking goes straight onto your Google Calendar, and you get a short email summary of every call the same day.
  • Call back — when a caller asks to be called back, or a call drops, Luna calls them back for you during your business hours. Anyone who says “stop” is never called again.
  • Sales support — when a caller asks about buying, Luna notes what they want and books a sales call with you at a time you choose. It qualifies the lead and hands it to you. Closing the sale stays your job.

Price: $149 a month if your business is in the United States, $59 a month anywhere else in the world. One price covers all five services. No setup fee. No per-call fee.

What happens next

  1. You tell Ting about your business — the details below, by email.
  2. Ting builds your receptionist from your own information and shows it to you before anything goes live. You check the words it uses. Nothing you have not approved goes to a caller.
  3. Your 14-day free trial runs on a trial setup that leaves your real phone number alone. No card required. If anything breaks or does not work as described during the trial, Ting fixes it free of charge.
  4. You decide. If you keep Luna, that is when you open your own Twilio account — you pay Twilio directly, about $1.15 a month for the number plus minutes at Twilio’s own rates. If you do not keep it, Ting takes the trial down and nothing is charged. You owe nothing either way.
